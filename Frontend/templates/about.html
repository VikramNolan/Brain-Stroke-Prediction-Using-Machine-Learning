ℹ️ About Stroke Prediction System

This system uses a trained Decision Tree model to predict the likelihood of stroke based on medical and lifestyle factors such as age, BMI, glucose level, smoking status, and more.

The model was trained on real clinical data and provides an indicative risk assessment. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Features Used:
Gender, Age, Hypertension, Heart Disease, Marital Status, Work Type, Residence Type, Average Glucose Level, BMI, Smoking Status.
